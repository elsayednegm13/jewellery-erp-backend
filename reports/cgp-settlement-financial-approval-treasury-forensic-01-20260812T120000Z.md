## 12. Atomicity and accounting contracts

The service wraps journal, `FinancialSettlement`, legs, `CashTransaction`, allocation, liability reduction, idempotency success, and audit in one Sequelize transaction. A failure rolls back the whole transaction. Approval-policy failure occurs before the idempotency claim and before any financial write.

`SETTLEMENT_ATOMICITY_MODEL = one database transaction for payment journal + settlement + legs + Treasury/CashTransactions + allocation + liability update + idempotency success + audit`

Cash contract:

`Dr CUSTOMER_CREDITOR (2500) / Cr CASH_TREASURY (SYS-CASH)`

Bank contract:

`Dr CUSTOMER_CREDITOR (2500) / Cr BANK_ACCOUNT (SYS-BANK)`; bank reference is required for a bank leg; no cash-session check applies to a bank-only leg.

Mixed contract:

One atomic settlement with separate CASH and BANK_TRANSFER legs; one policy evaluation uses payment method `MIXED`; total legs must not exceed outstanding; cash leg requires an OPEN session; all journal/Treasury/liability effects commit or roll back together.

`CASH_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNTING_CONTRACT = Dr CUSTOMER_CREDITOR / Cr SYS-CASH`

`BANK_SETTLEMENT_ACCOUNTING_CONTRACT = Dr CUSTOMER_CREDITOR / Cr SYS-BANK`

`MIXED_SETTLEMENT_CONTRACT = single atomic settlement, policy method MIXED, per-leg account/Treasury rows, cash session only for cash leg, total capped by outstanding`
